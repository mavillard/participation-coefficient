Maximum for five communities: 0.800

What's happening?

P(N) = 1 − Σ (connections to community / all connections)²

Balance matters: connections distributed equally across two communities give a higher coefficient than an unequal split.

The number of connections doesn't matter on its own: try 1:1:0:0:0, 5:5:0:0:0, and 10:10:0:0:0. All three give P = 0.500.

More communities allow a higher maximum: evenly connecting to two, three, four, or five communities gives P = 0.500, 0.667, 0.750, or 0.800, respectively.

Connecting to all five: 2:2:2:2:2 and 10:10:10:10:10 both yield P = 0.800. No configuration using only five communities can reach 0.99; that would require at least 100 evenly represented communities.

This explorer uses the standard unweighted participation coefficient. A node's own community is included in the calculation if it has neighbors there, just like any other community.
